## ① 情境：替一个小组核对证据

你的小组正在讨论大学图书馆是否应延长开放时间。一位同学说：“学生进馆次数增加了，所以延长时间一定提高了学习成绩。”你的任务不是凭常识赞成或反对，而是准确找出文章支持什么、没有测量什么，以及结论的适用范围。

Maya: The new hours clearly improved students' grades.

Leo: Which sentence gives evidence about grades?

Maya: The report says evening visits increased.

Leo: That supports a claim about library use. It doesn't necessarily support a claim about achievement.

Maya: So we should describe the change in visits and keep the conclusion narrower.

本课结束时，你应能用“对象—时间—比较—限制条件”四项检查定位结果，区分关键词相同与意思一致，并用原文证据解释一个判断。效率来自缩小查找范围，不来自跳过核对。

## ② 语法与阅读机制

**先取主干，再恢复限制。** 在 “Students who worked after class were more likely to use the late sessions” 中，主干是 Students were more likely to use the late sessions；who worked after class 限定所说的学生。不能把句意扩大成所有学生更常使用晚间时段。关系代词 who 指人，which 常指事物；that 可用于许多限定性关系从句。本课先识别意义，不要求机械替换所有关系代词。

**比较必须保留基准。** “Visits rose from 80 to 120” 的终值是 120，增量是 40；“rose by 40” 说明增量。数字若指 weekly visits，不能改写成 120 students：一个学生可能多次到访。Compared with the first four weeks, the second period recorded more visits. compared with 后面的基准不是结论的原因。

**连接词标出证据边界。** although 引导让步：“Although visits increased, the report did not measure learning.” 两个分句可以同时为真。because 给出原因，whereas 对照两个事实，therefore 引出推论；文章出现 therefore 也不意味着推论必然成立，仍需检查前提。

**范围与语气不能被转述吃掉。** may indicate = 可能提示；demonstrates = 证明/明确展示，力度通常更强。some respondents 不是 all students。did not measure satisfaction 与 found that satisfaction did not improve 不同：前者没有测量，后者测量后没有发现改善。

**指代回查。** “This change” 可能指上一整句描述的延时安排；不能默认只指距离最近的名词。把 this change 替换为你的理解，检查逻辑是否通顺。

对比例句与修正：

1. The survey included 60 respondents. → Sixty people answered the survey.（保持对象）
2. Sixty visits were recorded. ≠ Sixty different people visited.（次数与人数不同）
3. Some participants preferred quiet rooms. ≠ Participants generally rejected group work.（范围和态度都变了）
4. The schedule may have helped commuters. ≠ The schedule helped every commuter.（推测变成确定，部分变成全部）
5. The report did not compare grades. ≠ The report found identical grades.（未测量不等于没有差异）
6. Although demand rose, staffing stayed constant. → Staffing did not increase despite higher demand.（合理转述）
7. Visits increased by 40. → There were 40 more visits than before.（增量保持）
8. The room opened at nine, whereas the desk opened at ten.（比较开放时间，非因果）

练习：修正 “The second period had 120 new students, proving that late opening caused better grades.” 先不看答案，至少指出三个越界点。

## ③ 词汇与搭配

| 词/搭配 | 词性与中文意义 | 本课中的自然用法与辨析 |
|---|---|---|
| locate evidence | 动词短语，定位证据 | Locate evidence for the claim before accepting it. locate 强调查找位置；evaluate 是判断质量。 |
| a specific detail | 可数名词短语，具体细节 | The opening time is a specific detail. specific 不等于 important。 |
| a reference period | 可数名词短语，参照时段 | The first four weeks serve as the reference period. |
| record a visit | 动词短语，记录一次到访 | The gate records a visit each time someone enters. record 作动词，重音通常在后。 |
| a unique visitor | 可数名词短语，去重访客 | The counter cannot identify unique visitors. 与 visits 区分。 |
| survey respondents | 复数名词，问卷受访者 | Survey respondents volunteered to answer. 不代表所有在校学生。 |
| account for | 动词短语，解释/考虑到 | The analysis does not account for the approaching deadline. 本句是“考虑因素”，不是“占比”。 |
| an alternative explanation | 可数名词短语，另一种解释 | A deadline offers an alternative explanation for increased use. |
| remain constant | 系表结构，保持不变 | Staffing remained constant throughout the pilot. constant 不是 continually。 |
| a measurable outcome | 可数名词短语，可测量结果 | Course grades would be a measurable outcome. measurable 不意味着实际测量过。 |
| draw a distinction | 动词短语，作区分 | Draw a distinction between access and achievement. |
| support a claim | 动词短语，支持论断 | These figures support a claim about usage. 不自动等于 prove。 |
| impose a limit | 动词短语，施加限制 | The small sample imposes a limit on generalisation. |
| within the sample | 介词短语，在样本范围内 | The preference was common within the sample. |
| follow-up research | 不可数名词短语，后续研究 | Follow-up research could compare several campuses. research 通常不用 researches 指一般研究。 |
| a cautious interpretation | 名词短语，审慎解释 | A cautious interpretation retains the reported limitations. |

搭配练习：补全 locate ___；draw a distinction ___ X and Y；account ___ a competing explanation；remain ___；support a ___。答案在第⑥段。

## ④ 阅读与听力准备

### 原创阅读：What longer opening hours can—and cannot—tell us

**A.** Northbridge University's library committee wanted to know whether students needed access to study space later in the evening. The existing building closed at eight. Staff often saw students still working shortly before closing, but this observation could not show how many would stay if the building remained open. Rather than immediately changing the permanent schedule, the committee introduced an eight-week pilot. Its stated aim was to describe demand for evening study space, not to evaluate academic performance.

**B.** During the first four weeks, the building retained its normal closing time. In the next four, it stayed open until ten. The same entrance counter operated throughout the pilot. Average weekly visits between six and eight rose from 80 in the first period to 120 in the second. A separate average of 50 weekly visits was recorded between eight and ten during the extended-hours period. These numbers refer to entries, not different individuals: someone who left for food and returned was counted twice. The committee therefore avoided describing the total as the number of students served.

**C.** A voluntary questionnaire supplied a different kind of information. Sixty users responded during the final week. Thirty-six selected access to a quiet room as their main reason for coming, eighteen selected access to group-working space, and six selected access to printed books. Several respondents with paid jobs described the later closing time as convenient. However, people who did not visit the library were not invited to complete this questionnaire. Their preferences, including any reasons for staying away, remained unknown. The committee did not treat the answers as a representative survey of the whole university.

**D.** The timing of the pilot also complicated interpretation. A major coursework deadline fell in the final week, and students might have visited more often as that deadline approached even without a change in opening hours. No comparable building kept its original schedule while recording the same measures. Consequently, the report could describe the observed increase, but it could not confidently separate the effect of longer access from other influences. The absence of such a comparison was a limit on causal interpretation, not evidence that the extended schedule had no effect.

**E.** Practical constraints shaped the trial. Security coverage continued until ten, but the staffed information desk still closed at eight. Students could use the study rooms during the extra hours; they could not expect the same face-to-face assistance available earlier. The report recorded two complaints about this distinction. It did not measure whether the quieter late period helped students complete more work, nor did it compare course grades before and after the pilot. Satisfaction with access, time spent in a building and achievement were treated as separate questions requiring different evidence.

**F.** The committee recommended a second, longer pilot that would include weeks with and without major deadlines. It also proposed asking non-users about their needs and counting individual users in a way that protected privacy. This recommendation was conditional on staffing and budget approval. The first pilot had identified demand worth investigating; it had not established that permanent extended hours would be the best use of the university's resources. A useful report, the committee concluded, should make the limits of its evidence as easy to find as its headline figures.

### 定位步骤

先给每段写 3–6 字中文标签。然后圈出题目中的可定位锚点（时段、人数、比较对象），旁边写出可能的同义表达。扫读找到候选句后，必须读前后至少一句，检查代词和限制。最后只引用能支持答案的最小证据段，并说明为何排除干扰项。

**练习 A：段落匹配。** 找到下列具体信息，可重复选择段落；不要把段落大意当作细节定位。

1. 一个计数方式可能把同一人记录多次的原因。
2. 未参加问卷的一类人的观点仍不清楚。
3. 后续建议需要另行批准。
4. 一项与延时开放同时发生、可能影响到访的因素。
5. 建筑与服务台在关闭时间上的差别。

**练习 B：判断与解释。** 按文章信息写 True / False / Not Given，并标出证据。这里是自主练习，不计考试分数。

6. The average weekly number of visits between six and eight increased by 40.
7. Exactly 170 different students used the building each week during the second period.
8. Most questionnaire respondents selected quiet space as their main reason for visiting.
9. Students who did not use the library preferred studying at home.
10. The information desk remained staffed until ten during the pilot.
11. The committee concluded that longer hours had no effect on visits.
12. The university approved permanent extended hours.

**练习 C：精确转述。** 把以下不准确总结改写为 2–3 句：“A university experiment proved that students learn better when libraries stay open late. All students wanted quiet rooms, so the committee introduced permanent evening opening.” 保留确有证据的内容。

### 原创听力脚本（尚未录制）

Before you decide that two sentences mean the same thing, check four details. First, who or what was measured? Visits and visitors are different quantities. Second, which period is being described? A result from the last week may not apply to the entire pilot. Third, what is the comparison? An increase needs a starting point. Finally, what limit does the writer state? If a study did not measure grades, you cannot use its findings to claim that grades improved. Efficient reading means finding a likely location quickly and then checking the meaning carefully.

## ⑤ 发音与跟读准备

本课练习信息重音，而不是追求某个“口音总分”。比较：It counted **VISITS**, not **VISITORS**. 对比项需要清楚，介词和冠词可以较弱；不要把每个词都同样用力。record 作名词常为 /ˈrekɔːd/（英音），作动词为 /rɪˈkɔːd/；这里 “The gate records a visit” 使用动词。evidence /ˈevɪdəns/ 通常是不可数名词，读音中的重音在首音节。

按意群练习：The report described an INCREASE / but did not establish its CAUSE. It recorded ENTRIES / rather than UNIQUE visitors. The recommendation was CONDITIONAL / on staffing and budget approval.

先在纸上标重音，再录下自己的表达做自听：关键对比是否清楚？停顿是否切断名词词组？音频参考与人工试听尚待补齐；ASR 转写正确也不能证明每个音素正确，评估状态保持“待评估”。

## ⑥ 输出、示范与答案

**口语任务：** 用 60–90 秒向小组解释题 7 为什么不能成立。必须说清 entries / individuals 的区别，引用 B 段的重复计数机制，并避免反过来断言“肯定没有 170 人”。

示范：The figure describes visits rather than different students. Paragraph B explains that a person who leaves and comes back is counted again. We therefore cannot infer the number of unique users from the entrance totals. The passage gives evidence about how the counter works, but it does not supply an exact count of individuals.

**写作任务：** 写 130–170 词的委员会简报，至少包含一个准确数字、一个抽样限制和一个因果限制。以下为较短的结构示范，可扩写，不是唯一答案：

The pilot suggests that demand for evening study space deserves further investigation. Average weekly visits between six and eight increased from 80 to 120, while an additional 50 visits were recorded during the later period. These figures count entries rather than unique users. In the voluntary questionnaire, 36 of 60 respondents selected quiet space as their main reason for attending. However, the survey excluded non-users, and a coursework deadline may have influenced demand. The report therefore supports a cautious description of library use; it does not establish an improvement in academic achievement. A longer pilot should examine demand under different conditions before a permanent schedule is approved.

**答案及理由：**

- A1 B：离开买食物后再进入会被再次计数。A2 C：没有询问 non-users。A3 F：conditional on staffing and budget approval。A4 D：最后一周的 coursework deadline。A5 E：building 十点关，information desk 八点关。
- B6 True：120 − 80 = 40，时间范围和单位都是平均每周六至八点的 visits。B7 Not Given：文章没有去重人数；重复计数使 170 entries 无法用于推出 170 individuals，但也不能确定实际人数绝非170。B8 True：36/60 = 60%，超过一半；仅限回答问卷的人。B9 Not Given：原文说观点未知，没有给出在家学习偏好。B10 False：服务台八点关闭。B11 False：报告明确说不能分离原因，不是断定没有效果。B12 Not Given：建议有条件，文章没有说明最终批准或拒绝。
- C 示例：The pilot recorded increased evening visits but did not measure academic achievement. Quiet space was the most frequently selected reason among the 60 respondents, although non-users were not surveyed. The committee recommended a further pilot subject to staffing and budget approval.
- 第②段纠错：120 是平均每周到访次数，不是新学生人数；报告未测成绩；无法确立延时造成成绩变化的因果关系。
- 搭配：evidence；between；for；constant；claim。
- 听力准备：对象、时段、比较基准、作者声明的限制；研究没有测量成绩，因此无成绩改善证据。

自查：数字单位是否一致？是否擅自增加 all / always / prove？Not Given 是否只是未查到而非确实缺少信息？改写有没有保留限定条件？开放题允许不同表达，但必须可由原文支持。
