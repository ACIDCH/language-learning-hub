# English IELTS-style original lesson — Writing Task 2 U01: university online courses

**Status:** Draft sample index, not a licensed IELTS practice question. Full original learner-facing text is stored only in the private dedicated YUNPAN Google Drive English/IELTS-Academic folder.

## Six learning steps

1. **Context**: Universities offer online classes; analyse access, flexibility and quality.
2. **Grammar**: calibrated claims and limitations; because vs therefore; although clauses and concession; precise causation.
3. **Vocabulary**: academic support, access to education, student engagement, flexible scheduling, digital divide, peer collaboration, evidence-based argument and their authentic collocations.
4. **Reading and listening**: original mini-article and reasoned-comprehension prompts; licensed actual listening still pending.
5. **Pronunciation**: sentence stress and clarity in discussions; word-level green/yellow/orange/red/neutral only after validated engine. No total score.
6. **Application**: 60–90 second spoken opinion and an original 200–280-word written argument with optional AI revision comments.
