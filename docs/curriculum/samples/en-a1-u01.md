# English A1 — Unit 01: Greetings and identity

**Status:** original teaching sample for pedagogical review, not yet deployed. **Source anchor:** [VOA Let's Learn English Level 1](https://learningenglish.voanews.com/p/5644.html), [British Council A1-A2 Grammar](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/a1-a2). This sample is authored for this project; it does not reproduce VOA scripts.

## Goal / 学习目标

At the end of this lesson you can say your name, where you are from, what you do, and ask the same questions of another person. Suggested prerequisites: none. You are free to open any other unit.

## 1. In context / 情境导入

**Original sample dialogue**

> A: Hello! I'm Mia. What's your name?  
> B: Hi, I'm Leo. I'm from Wellington.  
> A: Nice to meet you, Leo. Are you a student?  
> B: Yes, I am. I'm an engineering student. What about you?  
> A: I'm a language student. Nice to meet you too.

中文提示：这是两位学生首次见面的交流。注意 `I am → I'm` 的缩写、问句中的 `Are you ...?` 以及回答中的 `Yes, I am.`。

## 2. Core grammar / 语法解释

**A. 人称代词与 be 动词**

| Subject 主语 | be | Common contraction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | am | I'm |
| You / We / They | are | you're / we're / they're |
| He / She / It | is | he's / she's / it's |

形式：`subject + am/is/are + complement`。身份和国籍通常用 be，不要把汉语的“是”逐字套用到所有动作动词。

**原创建模例句**

1. I am a student. — 我是一名学生。
2. I'm from New Zealand. — 我来自新西兰。
3. You are my classmate. — 你是我的同学。
4. She is French. — 她是法国人。
5. We are in the same class. — 我们在同一个班。
6. They are teachers. — 他们是老师。

**B. 否定句和一般疑问句**

- 否定：`He is not a teacher.` / `He isn't a teacher.`
- 疑问：`Are you a student?` / `Is she from Paris?`
- 肯定回答：`Yes, I am.`、`Yes, she is.`（短答一般不使用 `Yes, I'm.`）
- 否定回答：`No, I'm not.`、`No, she isn't.`

**典型错误对照**

- ❌ *She are a student.* → ✅ **She is a student.**（第三人称单数用 is）
- ❌ *You are from France?* → ✅ **Are you from France?**（标准初级一般疑问句用倒装；口语升调问句有语用例外）
- ❌ *I from Auckland.* → ✅ **I am from Auckland.**（需要 be 动词）
- ❌ *I am study English.* → ✅ **I study English.**（一般现在时动作动词不能与 am 直接连接）

## 4. Reception / 听力与阅读

**原创短文**

> Hello. My name is Nora. I am from Auckland, but my friend Max is from Paris. We are university students. Our English teacher is from Canada. She is friendly. We are in the same class.

理解任务（无考试与分数）：找出人物 Nora、Max、teacher 的身份及来自哪里。指出两个 `are` 的主语。

## 5. Pronunciation / 发音

- /ɪ/ vs /iː/: `sit` and `seat` as a listening contrast.
- Word stress: `STU-dent`, `ENG-lish`, `en-gi-NEER` (the final syllable is stressed in *engineer*).
- Yes/no question intonation: `Are you a student?` may rise for neutral confirmation; natural intonation is context dependent.
- Practice: `I'm from Auckland. Are you from France?`

## 6. Practice and production / 实践应用

**Self-check practice** (optional, no timed test)

1. Fill: `I ___ a student.`
2. Fill: `He ___ from Paris.`
3. Convert to negative: `They are teachers.`
4. Convert to a question: `She is from France.`
5. Correct: `We is from Auckland.`
6. Reply: `Are you a student?` (answer truthfully).

**Answer reference:** 1. am; 2. is; 3. They are not / aren't teachers; 4. Is she from France?; 5. We are from Auckland; 6. Yes, I am / No, I'm not (or truthful equivalent).

**Speak:** Record a natural 30-second self-introduction and ask two friendly questions. **Write:** 60–80 words introducing yourself, where you are from, and what you study or do. AI may offer non-scored grammar suggestions and additional original examples.
