# Content quality and limits

The source of truth is `config/course-completeness.json`, with all 108 IDs. Baseline 18 substantive drafts plus newly authored `en-ielts-reading-u02` = 19. The remaining 89 still need distinct full manuscripts. The twelve new French worksheets are supplements, not twelve additional complete lessons.

English Reading U02 introduces a fictional Northbridge library pilot. Questions distinguish repeated visits from unique visitors, a pre/post association from causation, and a proposal from an approved permanent change. Exercises have answers with evidence and include indeterminate claims; no unsupported IELTS band prediction is made. The text and all answer material are included in the public snapshot without truncation. The lesson's original spoken script is not labelled recorded audio.

French supplements cover tu/vous, s'appeler, country prepositions, possessive agreement, articles and negation, regular present-tense forms, question forms, aller/faire, partitives, directions, adjective agreement, futur proche and passé composé boundaries. Older source vocabulary is retained with contextual notes (for example Mademoiselle). U11's movement verbs are not misrepresented as a futur-proche dialogue. U12's source table contains two French example columns, not French/English translations.

All old public snapshots are hashed against the parent commit. Original Drive texts remain in place; a separate private full-text backup and source index preserve all 37 retrieved documents. No original grammar passage was silently deleted or corrected. Subsequent corrections should remain identifiable revisions with a semantic change explanation.

Open quality tasks: full per-unit exercise/answer counts for inherited masters, exact source links for every English lesson, full transcript-to-recording alignment, deeper higher-level content, human listening, professional linguistic review and approval. Automated passing checks establish structural integrity, not pedagogical approval.

Publication is rejected unless the same unit has reviewed six-section manuscript evidence, full lesson audio evidence and linguistic review evidence. Nine regression tests cover unsafe downloads/archives and unsupported completion claims.
